# What humans say. Edit freely: the game reads this file at start, and
# again when you press F5 while playing.
#
# [moment]        starts the lines for a moment. A number after the name is
#                 the chance (0 to 1) that anything is said at all, e.g.
#                 [hit 0.3]. Without one it is always said.
#
# Each row under it is one line, either just the words:
#     HELLO.
# or what it needs and its tone, then a bar, then the words:
#     hungry !friend rude | WHAT DO YOU WANT?
#
# A line is picked among those whose conditions all hold, the more
# conditions the likelier, so specific lines win over plain ones.
#
# Conditions about the speaker:
#   hungry starving scared hurt bleeding cakes keys
#   brave timid greedy generous curious homebody sociable loner
#   erratic guilty craving   (the mind: erratic, guilty over flesh eaten,
#                             craving more)
#   dazed                    (only just woken up, not clear-headed yet)
#   hopeless settled         (losing hope of a way out; given up and made
#                             a home)
#   sure                     (sure of its hunch of which way is out)
#   thirsty parched          (needing water; badly)
#   need.to.go bursting      (needing to piss or shit; about to burst)
#   filthy                   (feces or piss on it)
#   tired exhausted          (needing sleep; badly)
#   dreamt                   (woke from a dream of before lately)
#   again often              (saying something has happened again: the
#                             4th or 8th time; the 15th or 30th. Only in
#                             the *_again moments)
# about how the speaker sees the one it talks to:
#   friend foe liked disliked
#   suspect (thinks them a killer)  afraid (they hit it and it ran)
# about the one it talks to:
#   they.hungry they.starving they.scared they.hurt they.bleeding
#   they.cakes they.keys they.dazed they.thirsty they.parched they.filthy
#   they.asleep
# about the one spoken of, if dead, how far gone the body is:
#   body.fresh body.rotting body.bones
# Put ! in front to ask for the opposite: !hungry, !friend.
#
# Tones are what the line does to whoever hears it:
#   warm    the one spoken to trusts the speaker a little more
#   rude    the one spoken to trusts the speaker a little less
#   plead   the one spoken to may be moved to share a cake
#   fear    everyone in earshot who doesn't distrust the speaker gets scared
#   threat  the one threatened may hand over a cake, or else thinks worse
#           of the speaker; anyone else hearing it thinks a bit worse too
# No tone: the line just gets said.
#
# Names, filled in only when the speaker knows them (otherwise the line
# is skipped), and learnt by anyone hearing them who can see whose they are:
#   {NAME}  the one spoken to    {ME}  the speaker    {WHO}  someone else
#           (the dead, the looted, the one gossiped about)
#
# Keep lines short: the bubble is small. Everything shows in capitals.
# Never state the obvious: the player just saw what happened, so a line
# gives the feeling, not an account. "THEY WON'T TRUST ME NOW", not
# "I ROBBED THEM, THEY WON'T TRUST ME NOW".
# Each moment says who is speaking to whom.
