# Every sound in the game, made from a few numbers (src/audio/synth.h).
# F5 in the game reads this file again. `make soundsheet` renders each
# sound to build/sounds/NAME.wav to listen to.
#
#   crush BITS RATE     every sound after this is crushed to BITS bits a
#                       sample at RATE samples a second: the old-console sound
#   sound NAME [gain G] [bits B] [rate R]
#                       starts a sound; the code plays it by NAME
#   WAVE FROM [TO] [key value ...]
#                       a layer of the sound above. WAVE is square, triangle,
#                       saw, sine or noise. FROM and TO are Hz, slid from one
#                       to the other (noise takes a new value twice a period:
#                       low rumbles, high hisses). Keys, in seconds and Hz:
#                         gain      how loud, 0 to 1 (0.5)
#                         delay     wait before it starts (0)
#                         attack    rise (0.002)  hold  held (0)  decay  fall (0.1)
#                         lowpass   keep only what is under this (none)
#                         highpass  keep only what is over this (none)
#                         duty      a square's share high, 0 to 1 (0.5)
#                         repeat    played this many times (1), gap apart (0.1),
#                         gap fade  each fade times as loud as the last (1)
#                         wobble    pitch wandering at random, 0 to 1: crunch
#
# Heard through a wall, a sound is dulled before the crush.

crush 5 8000

# --- Feet. One step each time a foot comes down in the walk or run art.

sound step
    noise 1400 600 gain 0.5 decay 0.035 lowpass 2600
    square 140 70 gain 0.25 decay 0.03 duty 0.3

sound run gain 1.2
    noise 1800 700 gain 0.55 decay 0.04 lowpass 3000
    square 150 60 gain 0.35 decay 0.04 duty 0.3

# Fresh piss or poured water underfoot.
sound step_wet
    noise 2600 900 gain 0.5 attack 0.005 decay 0.08 lowpass 3500 highpass 400 wobble 0.4
    sine 500 260 gain 0.2 decay 0.05

# Wet blood underfoot: tacky, and a little suck as the foot lifts.
sound step_blood
    noise 900 400 gain 0.5 attack 0.004 decay 0.07 lowpass 1800 wobble 0.6
    sine 300 160 gain 0.3 decay 0.05
    noise 2400 1200 gain 0.2 delay 0.07 attack 0.01 decay 0.04 lowpass 3000

# Fresh feces underfoot.
sound step_filth
    noise 700 250 gain 0.6 attack 0.006 decay 0.1 lowpass 1300 wobble 0.8
    sine 180 90 gain 0.35 decay 0.08

# --- Voices.

# A bubble opening.
sound pop
    sine 520 900 gain 0.45 attack 0.003 decay 0.06
    square 1040 1800 gain 0.08 decay 0.03 duty 0.25

# A line said in fear: a short wail.
sound cry gain 0.9
    saw 620 420 gain 0.4 attack 0.02 hold 0.12 decay 0.25 lowpass 2200 wobble 0.15
    square 930 640 gain 0.12 attack 0.02 hold 0.1 decay 0.2 duty 0.2

# --- Doors.

# A key turned and a lock giving.
sound unlock
    square 1800 1500 gain 0.25 decay 0.02 duty 0.2
    noise 3000 gain 0.3 delay 0.06 decay 0.02 repeat 2 gap 0.04 fade 0.7
    square 220 160 gain 0.35 delay 0.13 decay 0.07 duty 0.4

# A server room's airlock: a hiss as it gives, and as it seals.
sound airlock_open
    noise 3600 2200 gain 0.35 attack 0.03 hold 0.1 decay 0.25 highpass 800
    square 90 70 gain 0.2 decay 0.12

sound airlock_shut
    noise 2400 3600 gain 0.3 attack 0.02 hold 0.06 decay 0.15 highpass 800
    square 110 55 gain 0.45 delay 0.2 decay 0.12 duty 0.4
    noise 400 gain 0.3 delay 0.2 decay 0.1 lowpass 900

# A slam room's gate falling: a heavy bang and its ring.
sound slam gain 1.2
    noise 600 120 gain 0.9 decay 0.35 lowpass 1600
    square 70 38 gain 0.7 decay 0.4 duty 0.45
    triangle 330 320 gain 0.18 delay 0.03 attack 0.01 decay 0.7

# --- Traps.

# Spikes up through a foot: steel, then the wet of it.
sound spikes gain 1.1
    square 2600 1900 gain 0.3 decay 0.12 duty 0.15
    noise 5000 1500 gain 0.45 decay 0.08 highpass 1200
    noise 800 250 gain 0.6 delay 0.05 attack 0.01 decay 0.22 lowpass 1500 wobble 0.9
    sine 220 90 gain 0.35 delay 0.06 decay 0.18

# A dart let fly, and one going in.
sound dart_fire
    noise 4000 1800 gain 0.35 attack 0.01 decay 0.1 highpass 1500
    square 900 400 gain 0.12 decay 0.06 duty 0.2

sound dart_hit
    noise 1600 600 gain 0.55 decay 0.06 lowpass 2400
    noise 700 300 gain 0.4 delay 0.03 attack 0.01 decay 0.12 lowpass 1200 wobble 0.8

# --- Violence.

# A blow landing: a thud with a crunch in it, and something wet.
sound hit gain 1.1
    square 120 50 gain 0.6 decay 0.12 duty 0.4
    noise 1300 400 gain 0.6 decay 0.09 lowpass 2400 wobble 0.7
    noise 3200 2000 gain 0.25 delay 0.01 decay 0.03 highpass 1000 repeat 3 gap 0.02 fade 0.6
    noise 500 200 gain 0.25 delay 0.07 attack 0.01 decay 0.12 lowpass 900 wobble 1

# Someone falling dead: the body down, then a last wet breath.
sound death gain 1.1
    square 90 40 gain 0.6 decay 0.3 duty 0.45
    noise 500 120 gain 0.7 decay 0.35 lowpass 1100
    noise 900 300 gain 0.3 delay 0.35 attack 0.08 hold 0.15 decay 0.5 lowpass 1200 wobble 1
    saw 140 70 gain 0.15 delay 0.35 attack 0.1 hold 0.1 decay 0.5 lowpass 600 wobble 0.4
