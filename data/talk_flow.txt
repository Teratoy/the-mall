# How the talk fits together, for the DIAGRAM tab of the lines editor
# (make lines). Nothing in the game reads this file: it only says, for
# each moment in data/lines.txt, what makes a human say it, what saying it
# does, and which moments can come next. Edit freely, here or in the tab.
#
# [moment] who      starts a moment's section. Who it is said to:
#                     someone   the one it talks to
#                     nobody    said to itself, or to the room
#                     shout     shouted, heard far off
#                     wall      called through a wall or a locked door
#                   then "about" if it is about someone else (the dead,
#                   the one gossiped about, the one it blames).
#
# Under it, any of these rows, as many as needed:
#     when | what makes it say this, in plain words, and what has to hold
#     does | what saying it changes, beyond the tone of the line said:
#            trust, what the hearer knows, a cake handed over, a fight
#     -> moment chance | when it leads there
#            a moment that can come next, said by either of them, and how
#            often: always often sometimes seldom
#
# The tones' own effects (warm, rude, plead, fear, threat) are in the head
# of data/lines.txt and need not be repeated here.
#
# The tab also shows what the code says: where each moment is said, the
# comments there, and the follows it can see. It flags moments with no
# section here, branches to moments that don't exist, and moments with no
# "when" that nothing leads to. None of that fails make check.

[meet] someone
when | Greeting someone it has not met, once both are willing and close
does | Both greet; each then may tell the other news, ask the way, give a name
-> introduce sometimes | each decides whether to say who it is; more for the sociable and friends
-> tell_cake sometimes | it knows a cake the hungry, cakeless other doesn't (not a stingy one)
-> tell_key sometimes | the other has no key and it knows one they don't
-> tell_trap sometimes | it knows a trap they don't
-> tell_door sometimes | news of the way to a locked door
-> warn sometimes | trusted both ways, it has a bad opinion of a third person worth passing on
-> praise sometimes | the same, with a good opinion
-> ask_way seldom | it is set on getting out and doesn't take them for a foe
-> lie sometimes | a cunning one greeting a foe it can bait

[catch_up] someone
when | Greeting someone met before, after a while apart, when it wants a word or has news
does | Greets, and trust goes up a little
-> introduce sometimes | if they still don't know its name
-> tell_cake sometimes | it knows a cake the hungry, cakeless other doesn't
-> tell_key sometimes | the other has no key and it knows one
-> tell_trap sometimes | it knows a trap they don't
-> warn sometimes | confiding, with an opinion of a third person to pass on
-> praise sometimes | confiding, with a good word to pass on
-> ask_way seldom | it is set on getting out and doesn't take them for a foe

[introduce] someone
when | Greeting, it decides to say who it is; a friend always, others by chance, sociable more
when | More likely when the other has just given their name
does | The other learns its name, and so does anyone who sees whose it is

[tell_cake] someone
when | Hungry other with no cake, and it knows a cake they don't; friends, or if it isn't stingy
does | The hearer learns where the cake is, trusts the teller more, and rethinks what to do
-> thank_tip always | when the hearer learns something new

[tell_key] someone
when | The other has no key and it knows where one is that they don't know of
does | The hearer learns the key's place and trusts the teller more
-> thank_tip always | when it is news to the hearer

[tell_trap] someone
when | It knows a trap the other doesn't, and isn't selling it
does | The hearer learns the trap; trust goes up
-> thank_tip always | when it is news to the hearer

[tell_door] someone
when | It knows the way to a locked door the other hasn't seen
does | The hearer learns the route and its cells; trust goes up
-> thank_tip always | when it is news to the hearer

[sell_trap] someone
when | A greedy one, not a friend, tells of a trap only to someone with a cake to spare
when | Cunning ones sometimes make the trap up to sell
does | The hearer learns the trap, or a false one, and now owes a cake
-> thank_tip always | when the hearer learns it
-> pay sometimes | the buyer still has a cake and is there when the greeting ends

[lie] someone
when | A cunning one, to a foe: a way over hidden spikes made to sound like the way to a door
does | The hearer takes it for a true route; walking into the spikes will show it was a lie
-> thank_tip always | the foe believes it
-> spiked sometimes | the foe follows the route over the plate

[warn] someone about
when | Trusted both ways, it has a bad opinion of a third person the other hasn't heard
does | The hearer hears the opinion and may learn the name; trust of the teller goes up

[praise] someone about
when | Trusted both ways, it has a good opinion of a third person the other hasn't heard
does | The hearer hears the opinion and may learn the name; trust of the teller goes up

[thank_tip] someone
when | Just told something new: a cake, a key, a trap, a door; lies too
does | If the tip was sold, it owes for it as the greeting ends

[refuse] someone
when | Someone comes to greet a foe, a suspect, or one it is afraid of
does | No greeting happens; the one turned away leaves it be for a while
-> snubbed often | the one turned away answers back

[snubbed] someone
when | Turned away out loud, the one who came to greet may answer back

[beg] someone
when | Hungry with no cake and none known on the floor (unless starving), asks a cake holder it sees
when | Shyer, less sociable or greedier ones beg less; the starving beg more
does | It waits in place for the answer
does | A plea may move the holder to share, once per plea
-> give sometimes | the holder is moved, more if sociable, not greedy or a friend
-> thanks sometimes | after the cake is given

[give] someone
when | Handing a cake to someone who begged, or to a starving one it would feed
does | The cake changes hands and the other's trust goes up; they are let be
-> thanks always | the one fed thanks it

[thanks] someone
when | Given a cake, or water, by someone
does | The giver is trusted more

[pay] someone
when | A tip bought is paid for as the greeting ends, if still there and with a cake
does | The cake is handed over, trust goes up

[threat] someone
when | Hungry after a cake holder it is set to fight, near enough, with no grudge yet
when | Braver and greedier ones do it more
does | It waits in place for the answer; the target may hand over or refuse, and others think worse of it
-> yield often | the threatened has a cake, isn't starving or after it, and is timid or frightened enough
-> defy sometimes | otherwise; trust drops
-> hit often | the cake is not handed over and the fight begins

[yield] someone
when | Threatened, not in a fight or running, with a cake in range of the threatener and not starving
does | It hands a cake to the threatener, who drops the fight and is held in the wrong

[defy] someone
when | Threatened and not handing anything over
does | Trust of the threatener drops further
-> hit often | the threatener goes on to fight for the cake

[hit] someone
when | Landing a punch on the one it fights, whether for cakes, water, a grudge or to eat
does | The blow hurts, the victim's trust drops and it takes a cake or bottle if it can
-> ouch always | the victim cries out
-> bottle_taken sometimes | it takes the victim's bottle when thirsty
-> flee often | the victim, if hurt or not hungry, runs
-> killed sometimes | the blow kills

[ouch] someone
when | Hit by another
does | Trust of the hitter drops and it is scared; if asleep it wakes with a start
-> flee often | not hungry or too hurt, and not brave enough to stand
-> give sometimes | hit by a starving one it would feed anyway, it gives the cake
-> hit sometimes | hungry or cornered, it fights back

[flee] shout
when | Running from whoever hit it
does | Carried to anyone who can hear, not the one it runs from; those who aren't distrusted are scared

[killed] nobody about
when | Beaten to death by its hand, seen by it; one that bled out out of sight is only learnt at the body
does | It remembers killing; those who saw it remember too
-> loot sometimes | someone goes through the body

[darted] nobody
when | Hit by a dart from the wall
does | Scared, wounded, remembers the trap and grows wary of darts

[spiked] nobody
when | Steps on spikes
does | Scared, wounded in the foot or leg and bleeding hard; remembers the trap

[slammed] nobody
when | Shut in a slam room by a closing gate
does | Scared; remembers the trap and grows wary of slam rooms
-> help sometimes | with no key it cries out now and then through the walls
-> help_steps sometimes | it hears steps beyond the wall

[help] wall
when | Shut in a slam room with no key, now and then
does | Anyone close enough hears it and may set off to let them out; it starts banging on the gate
-> help_coming sometimes | a hearer with a key, more if kind or a friend
-> freed sometimes | someone opens the gate

[help_coming] nobody about
when | Heard someone shut in, and set off to let them out, with a key
does | It means to go to the gate and let them out
-> freed sometimes | it opens the gate

[freed] someone
when | Let out of a slam room by someone
does | Trusts the one who opened it more, hope lifts; with no key, it follows them

[found_body] nobody about
when | Seeing a dead body it hasn't noted before
does | Scared; remembers the body and may suspect or blame a bloody one it just saw
-> blame sometimes | it saw someone bloody lately and ties them to the body
-> gnawed sometimes | the body has been eaten from
-> loot sometimes | someone goes through the body

[loot] nobody about
when | Taking keys, cakes and bottles off a body
does | Seen doing it, they think worse of it
-> shame often | the nearest who saw it, not a foe of the dead

[shame] someone about
when | Seeing someone go through a body, it says so to them
does | Trust of the looter drops

[pool] nobody
when | Sees a fresh pool of blood that isn't its own or one it spilled
does | Scared; the first stares at it; keeps off it for good

[own_pool] nobody
when | Seeing a fresh pool of its own blood, near enough
does | It remembers the pool

[escort] someone
when | Not hungry, sociable, with a cake, seeing someone wounded and bloody it doesn't distrust
does | It leads them to the nearer known water and waits while they wash
-> escorted always | the wounded one answers

[escorted] someone
when | Led to water by another
does | It goes with them to the water, and washes or drinks

[accuse] someone
when | Seeing someone near caked in blood with no wound, heavy enough to be damning
does | Trust of them drops and it is scared
-> blame sometimes | seen near a body it found just now

[learn_cakes] nobody
when | Gone starving with no cake in hand
does | It keeps more cakes from now on and fetches them sooner
-> learn_again seldom | when the lesson was held and it is learnt again

[learn_keys] nobody
when | At a dead end at a locked door, with no key
does | From now on it fetches keys first
-> learn_again seldom | when the lesson was held and it is learnt again

[snap] nobody
when | The mind gives way from starving, thirst, dread or despair
does | From now on it is erratic
-> hunt sometimes | erratic and hungry, with someone to eat in sight

[hunt] someone
when | Going for someone to kill and eat them, once, when it first picks them
does | The fight begins; they are chased wherever they run
-> hit often | it catches them

[feed] nobody about
when | Eating from a body for the first time
does | Guilt or craving grows; feeds trust of anyone who sees it down
-> saw_eat often | anyone who sees it feeding
-> sick sometimes | the flesh was rotten

[sick] nobody
when | The flesh it ate was rotten
does | Health drops and it is sick for a time

[saw_eat] someone about
when | Seeing someone eat a body, the first time for that eater
does | Scared; their trust drops hard; the timid keep away and a brave friend of the dead goes for them
-> flee sometimes | the timid run from the eater
-> hit sometimes | a brave friend of the dead goes for them

[gnawed] nobody about
when | Seeing a half-eaten body, the first time, not mere bones
does | Scared, and the mind slips a little

[haunted] nobody about
when | Seeing again a body it ate from, while guilt is heavy enough
does | Scared by it; no more than every so often

[craving] nobody about
when | Hungry, craving flesh, at the sight of a body with meat left
does | No more than every so often

[wake] nobody
when | Waking up in the mall, remembering nothing, as it sits up
-> wake_stand always | when it first gets to its feet, still in a fog

[first_key] nobody
when | Sees a key for the first time; once only
when | Free to (not hungry, thirsty, tired, fleeing) it walks up to it first, then says it
does | Dropped if the key is gone before it gets there

[first_cake] nobody
when | Sees a cake for the first time; once only
when | Free to, it goes and looks at it first, and remarks on it there
does | Dropped if the cake is gone before it gets there

[first_door] nobody
when | Sees a door for the first time; once only
when | Free to, it walks up to it before remarking on it

[first_water] nobody
when | Sees water it could drink for the first time; once only
when | Free to, it walks up to it before remarking on it

[first_human] nobody
when | Sees another human for the first time; said where it stands, not walked up to
when | Once only, however many it meets after

[first_prints] nobody about
when | Sees someone else's footprints in feces for the first time; once only
when | Needs light to see them; free to, it goes and looks first
does | Names whose prints they are, if it knows them

[first_toilet] nobody
when | Sees a toilet for the first time; once only
when | Free to, it walks up to it before remarking on it

[despair] nobody
when | Its hope drops below the gloomy mark: starting to think there is no way out
when | The timid lose hope faster, the fearless slower
does | Remembers it as an episode of despair, and the gloom slows its chatting

[hope] nobody
when | Its hope climbs back above the gloomy mark after despair
does | Remembers it as an episode; it wants out again

[settle] nobody
when | Hope runs out (the erratic give up sooner): it gives up on a way out and makes a home
does | Picks a home by water, where it sleeps safely, or by a table; stops looking for the way
does | Tells no one; a settler later answers "the way" with gloom
-> guard sometimes | when someone comes into its place and it is brave or grasping

[guard] someone
when | A settler who is brave or grasping sees a stranger it doesn't trust come into its place
when | Not said if it let them drink there for a cake, or told them off in the drink exchange
does | Thinks less of the intruder; if they stay past the grace time it goes for them
-> hit sometimes | when the intruder stays on after the warning
-> refuse_drink sometimes | when the intruder came for its water

[ask_way] someone
when | Still wanting out, at a greeting, it sometimes asks one it doesn't take for a foe the way out
when | Asks each person only once; or asks back after being answered
does | The other answers at once
-> tell_way often | when the other has a hunch it feels sure enough of
-> no_way_out sometimes | when the other is settled or gloomy
-> lie_way sometimes | when the other is a cunning foe
-> no_idea often | when the other is too unsure, or won't say
-> ask_way sometimes | when the answerer wants out too, it asks back

[tell_way] someone
when | Answering "which way out", with a hunch it is sure enough of
does | Turns and points the way as it says it
does | Pulls the hearer's hunch toward it, as far as they trust the teller; lifts their hope
-> ask_way sometimes | when the answerer would ask back

[no_way_out] someone
when | Answering "which way out" when it has settled or is gloomy; told however unsure it is
does | Spreads gloom: the hearer's hope falls by how sure the teller sounds
does | The hearer is not told again unless something changed
-> ask_way sometimes | when the answerer would ask back

[lie_way] someone
when | A cunning foe is asked the way: it points the opposite way, or at a bait trap
does | The hearer's hunch is bent; if it points at spikes the hearer can learn it was a lie

[no_idea] someone
when | Asked the way out when its hunch is too unsure and it has nothing gloomy or false to say
-> ask_way sometimes | when the answerer would ask back

[join] someone
when | Free of company, a sociable human who knows someone in sight keeps with them
when | A stranger only if sociable and it trusts them enough; never a foe, suspect, or someone filthy
does | Starts keeping with them: follows and waits for them from now on
-> join_reply always | the other answers out loud

[join_reply] someone
when | Answering someone who just said they'd keep with it
does | Its tone shows if it welcomes or rebuffs the company

[follow] someone
when | After a greeting, with no key of its own, it finds the new friend has one
when | Not for a foe or one it shuns
does | Takes them as its leader and follows them
-> part_own_key sometimes | when it picks up or heads for a key of its own
-> part_no_keys sometimes | when their keys run out
-> part_lost sometimes | when it loses sight of them

[part_lost] nobody about
when | Keeping with or following someone, it reaches where it last saw them and they are gone
does | Stops keeping with them

[part_turned] someone
when | Leaving one it keeps with or follows, now it calls them a foe, a suspect or doesn't trust them
does | Leaves them; the one left thinks the worse of it

[part_filthy] someone
when | Keeping with someone it sees has become filthy, it leaves until they wash
does | Stops keeping with them

[part_no_keys] someone
when | Following someone who it sees now has no keys left, who is alive and not shunned
does | Stops following them

[part_own_key] someone
when | Following a leader, it picks up a key, or sets off to fetch one
does | Stops following the leader

[chat_here] someone
when | An urge to talk builds with someone it knows in sight, within a few cells, both calm
when | The urge grows faster in the sociable and with friends; slower when dreading or gloomy
does | Raises something in sight; the other answers from memory
-> reply_agree often | when the other thinks the same
-> reply_add sometimes | when it knows more of it, such as a friend's body
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[chat_shared] someone about
when | Chat urge fires and the thing the two both remember matters most to it
does | The other tells its own memory of the same thing
-> reply_agree always | whatever the other remembers, unless it brushes it off
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[chat_story] someone about
when | Chat urge fires and what only it went through or saw matters most to it
-> reply_add sometimes | when the other has been through the same kind of thing
-> reply_unheard sometimes | when the other has not
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[chat_someone] someone about
when | Chat urge fires and someone else, out of earshot, comes to its mind
does | Gives its opinion of them to the other
-> reply_agree sometimes | when they think alike of that person
-> reply_disagree sometimes | when they think opposite of them
-> reply_unheard often | when the other doesn't know them or has no firm view
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[chat_feeling] someone
when | Chat urge fires and how it feels (peckish, dry, footsore, uneasy, gloomy) stands out
-> reply_agree sometimes | when the other feels the same
-> reply_add sometimes | when the other feels the opposite and says something kind
-> reply_disagree seldom | for gloom, when the other is hopeful
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[chat_way] someone
when | Chat urge fires and the way out is what it thinks of
-> reply_agree sometimes | when the other's hunch points the same way
-> reply_disagree sometimes | when it points elsewhere, or the other is gloomy
-> reply_unheard sometimes | when the other has no hunch worth the name
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[chat_group] someone
when | Chat urge fires between two who keep together, about the group they are in
-> reply_agree always | unless the other brushes it off
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[chat_before] someone
when | Chat urge fires and the lack of any memory from before is on its mind
-> reply_agree always | unless the other brushes it off
-> reply_brush sometimes | when the other is a loner, uneasy, or distrusts the speaker

[reply_agree] someone
when | Answering a chat: it remembers or thinks the same, or something in sight is plain to both
does | The topic's other side is told back to the one who raised it
-> chat_follow sometimes | when both are sociable, so the chat runs on
-> chat_close seldom | after a follow-up, when they are friends or both very sociable

[reply_add] someone
when | Answering a chat: it has been through the same kind of thing, or has its own side to add
-> chat_follow sometimes | when both are sociable, so the chat runs on
-> chat_close seldom | after a follow-up, when they are friends or both very sociable

[reply_disagree] someone
when | Answering a chat: it thinks otherwise of the person, the way out, or hope
-> chat_follow sometimes | when both are sociable, so the chat runs on
-> chat_close seldom | after a follow-up, when they are friends or both very sociable

[reply_unheard] someone
when | Answering a chat: it knows nothing of it, or has no firm view
-> chat_follow sometimes | when both are sociable, so the chat runs on
-> chat_close seldom | after a follow-up, when they are friends or both very sociable

[reply_brush] someone
when | Answering a chat while uneasy, a loner it doesn't count as a friend, or distrusting the speaker
does | Ends the chat: there is no follow-up or close

[chat_follow] someone
when | The one who raised a chat remarks on the answer, if the chat runs a third turn
when | Only when both are sociable enough, and the answer was not a brush-off
-> chat_close sometimes | when they are friends or both very sociable

[chat_close] someone
when | The answerer closes a chat that runs a fourth turn
when | Only between friends or two very sociable humans, never after a brush-off

[parched] nobody

[foul_water] nobody

-> foul_warned seldom | when it drinks foul water it was warned of

[need_to_go] nobody

[accident] nobody

-> mock sometimes | when one who saw is cruel (grasping and not sociable)

[stepped_in] nobody

[seen_going] someone

[into_water] someone

[privacy] someone

[toilet_wait] someone

[bloody_water] nobody

[fouled] someone
when | Sees someone wash in water it is thirsty for, or lives by (settled near it)
when | Not said by the erratic; only once a minute per washer
does | Thinks less of the washer, more if it was washed in its home water
-> my_water seldom | the washer may then be turned from the water when it comes to drink

[my_water] someone
when | A settler is come upon by someone thirsty at the water by its home, and won't share
when | Not for a friend; the greedy won't let a cakeless or non-parched stranger drink
does | Turns them away; they keep off that water for a while
-> snubbed seldom | the turned-away may answer back

[water_free] someone
when | A settler lets someone thirsty drink its home water: the sociable, or the not greedy if they are parched
does | They may drink there for a minute or so, free
-> thanks sometimes | the one let drink is grateful

[water_price] someone
when | A greedy settler asks a thirsty stranger with a cake for it, at its home water
does | They may drink; they owe it a cake when done drinking
-> pay often | the drinker pays a cake after drinking
-> water_cheat sometimes | the drinker, bold and greedy, walks off without paying

[water_cheat] someone
when | The one who drank its water for a price, and didn't pay, is still in its sight
does | Thinks much less of them and sets on them in a fight
-> hit often | the guard goes for the cheat

[tell_water] someone

-> thank_tip always | the hearer thanks the one who told

[sell_water] someone
when | At a greeting, the grasping tell a thirsty stranger with a cake the way to water, for a cake

-> thank_tip always | the buyer thanks them
-> pay often | the buyer pays a cake at the end of the greeting, if still near

[lie_water] someone

-> thank_tip always | the foe thanks them, taken in

[ask_water] someone

-> tell_water often | one of the group knows a way and tells it
-> no_water sometimes | the one asked knows of none and says so

[lead_drink] someone

[led_drink] someone

[thirsty] nobody

[learn_water] nobody

[toilet_drink] nobody

-> toilet_mock sometimes | a greedy, unsociable onlooker mocks it

[toilet_mock] someone

[tired] nobody

[sleep] nobody

[woke] nobody
