# What each condition and tone in data/lines.txt means, in plain words, for
# the lines editor. Written from what src/human/talk.cpp checks (holds and
# holdsTopic). A condition the game has but this file lacks shows in the
# editor as "no description yet": add it here.
#
#   name | what it means
#   ## Heading        starts a group in the editor's list
# "It" is the speaker, "they" the one it speaks to. "!" in front of a
# condition asks for the opposite; the editor says so itself.

## The speaker's body and needs
hungry        | It is hungry (hunger at half or more).
starving      | It is starving (hunger at 80% or more). Always hungry too.
thirsty       | It is thirsty (thirst at half or more).
parched       | It is parched (thirst at 80% or more). Always thirsty too.
tired         | It is tired enough to want sleep.
exhausted     | It is exhausted: it walks slower, and soon drops asleep where it stands. Always tired too.
hurt          | It is hurt: below half its health.
bleeding      | It has a wound that is still bleeding.
need.to.go    | It needs to piss or shit and can't put it off.
bursting      | Its bladder or bowels are about to burst (90% full or more).
filthy        | It has feces or piss on it.
scared        | It is frightened right now.
dazed         | It has only just woken up in the mall and isn't clear-headed yet.
dreamt        | It woke from a dream of before not long ago.
cakes         | It carries at least one cake.
keys          | It carries at least one key.

## The speaker's nature (traits; about a third of humans each way)
brave         | It is brave.
timid         | It is timid (the opposite of brave).
greedy        | It is greedy.
generous      | It is generous (the opposite of greedy).
curious       | It is curious: it likes to explore.
homebody      | It is a homebody (the opposite of curious).
sociable      | It is sociable: it seeks others out.
loner         | It is a loner (the opposite of sociable).

## The speaker's mind
erratic       | Its mind has given way: it acts erratically and may go for flesh.
guilty        | It feels guilty over flesh it has eaten.
craving       | It craves more flesh.
hopeless      | It is losing hope of ever finding a way out.
settled       | It has given up on getting out and made itself a home.
sure          | It feels sure of its hunch about which way is out.

## Something happening again (only in the *_again moments)
again         | It is saying how it feels that this has happened again: the 4th or 8th time.
often         | It is saying how it feels that this keeps happening: the 15th or 30th time.

## How the speaker sees them
friend        | It counts them as a friend.
foe           | It counts them as a foe, and lets it show (a cunning speaker hides it).
liked         | It likes them a little or more. A friend is always liked too.
disliked      | It dislikes them a little or more, and lets it show (a cunning speaker hides it).
suspect       | It thinks they're a killer, from the blood on them.
afraid        | It is afraid of them: they hit it and it ran.

## Them, the one spoken to
they.hungry   | They are hungry.
they.starving | They are starving.
they.thirsty  | They are thirsty.
they.parched  | They are parched.
they.scared   | They are frightened right now.
they.hurt     | They are hurt: below half their health.
they.bleeding | They have a wound that is still bleeding.
they.cakes    | They carry at least one cake.
they.keys     | They carry at least one key.
they.dazed    | They have only just woken up and aren't clear-headed yet.
they.filthy   | They have feces or piss on them.
they.asleep   | They are asleep (it's talking to a sleeper).

## The body talked about ({WHO}), when that one is dead
body.fresh    | The body is fresh.
body.rotting  | The body has started to rot, but isn't bones yet.
body.bones    | Only bones are left.

## Chit-chat: what the topic is (in chat moments only)
is.here       | The topic is something in sight (chat_here).
is.shared     | The topic is something both of them went through or saw (chat_shared).
is.story      | The topic is something only the speaker went through or saw (chat_story).
is.someone    | The topic is someone else, out of earshot (chat_someone).
is.feeling    | The topic is how the speaker is doing (chat_feeling).
is.way        | The topic is the way out (chat_way).
is.group      | The topic is the two of them keeping together (chat_group).
is.before     | The topic is the life before waking that nobody remembers (chat_before).

## Chit-chat: something in sight
here.statue   | It's a giant statue ({THING} is it, e.g. BRONZE MUG).
here.water    | It's a pool or canal of water.
here.basin    | It's a basin of water.
here.body     | It's a dead body.
here.blood    | It's a pool of blood.
here.mess     | It's piss or feces on the floor.
foul          | The water or basin in sight is one it knows is fouled.
feces         | The mess in sight is feces, not piss.
statue.mug     | The statue is a mug.
statue.duck    | The statue is a rubber duck.
statue.chair   | The statue is a chair.
statue.teapot  | The statue is a teapot.
statue.toilet  | The statue is a toilet.
statue.toaster | The statue is a toaster.
statue.lamp    | The statue is a desk lamp.
statue.clock   | The statue is an alarm clock.
statue.spoon   | The statue is a spoon.
statue.bulb    | The statue is a light bulb.

## Chit-chat: what happened (for shared things and stories)
ep.fight      | It was a fight.
ep.kill       | Someone was killed.
ep.body       | A body was found.
ep.flesh      | Someone ate from a body.
ep.gnawed     | A half-eaten body was found.
ep.trap       | A trap went off.
ep.spotted    | A hidden trap was spotted before it went off.
ep.gift       | Someone gave someone a cake.
ep.door       | Someone unlocked a door.
ep.place      | They came into a part of the mall new to the speaker.
ep.pool       | A fresh pool of blood was found.
ep.escort     | Someone led someone to water.
ep.snapped    | The speaker's mind snapped, or came back (dark tells which).
ep.friend     | The speaker came to think of someone as a friend.
ep.foe        | The speaker came to think of someone as a foe.
ep.lied       | The speaker found out someone lied to it.
ep.starving   | The speaker went starving.
ep.parched    | The speaker went parched.
ep.loot       | Someone went through a body's things.
ep.foul       | The speaker drank foul water.
ep.despair    | The speaker lost hope, or got it back (dark tells which).
ep.settled    | The speaker made itself a home.
ep.freed      | Someone let someone out of a slam room.
by.me         | The speaker is the one who did it.
to.me         | The speaker is the one it was done to.
by.you        | The listener is the one who did it.
to.you        | The listener is the one it was done to.
dark          | The mind snapping or hope lost, as against coming back (with ep.snapped or ep.despair).
strong        | It struck the speaker hard.
recent        | It happened within the last minute.
spikes        | The trap was spikes.
darts         | The trap was darts.
slam          | The trap was a slam room (shut in).
gift.share    | The cake was shared freely.
gift.pay      | The cake was paid for.
gift.yield    | The cake was handed over under threat.

## Chit-chat: someone else
about.dead     | The one talked about is dead, and the speaker knows it.
about.liked    | The speaker thinks well of the one talked about.
about.disliked | The speaker thinks badly of the one talked about.
about.friend   | The one talked about is the speaker's friend.
about.foe      | The one talked about is the speaker's foe.
they.know      | The listener has met the one talked about.

## Chit-chat: feelings
feel.peckish   | It's getting hungry, not yet looking for food.
feel.dry       | It's getting thirsty.
feel.footsore  | It has walked a long while without a rest.
feel.uneasy    | It feels dread.
feel.gloomy    | It's losing hope.
feel.hopeful   | It's sure there's a way out.

## Chit-chat: keeping together
i.lead         | The speaker leads: the listener keeps with it.
you.lead       | The listener leads: the speaker keeps with them.

## Chit-chat: how the opener was answered (in follow-ups and closes)
agreed         | The answer agreed: it remembered or thought the same.
added          | The answer added something of its own.
disagreed      | The answer thought otherwise.
unheard        | The answer knew nothing of it.

## Tones: what the line does to whoever hears it
plain          | No tone: the line is just said.
warm           | The one spoken to trusts the speaker a little more.
rude           | The one spoken to trusts the speaker a little less.
plead          | The one spoken to may be moved to share a cake.
fear           | Everyone in earshot who doesn't distrust the speaker gets scared.
threat         | The one threatened may hand over a cake, or else thinks worse of the speaker; anyone else hearing it thinks a bit worse too.
